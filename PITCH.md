# JETSYNC AI

## Product Summary
JETSYNC AI is the AI-powered second-screen experience for New York Jets fans. It turns a fragmented game-day routine into a single personalized fan journey built around live game intelligence, fantasy guidance, social connection, and immersive celebration moments.

## The Problem
Jets fans are split across multiple experiences during every game:
- TV for live action
- Stats apps for game flow
- Fantasy tools for lineup decisions
- Message threads for fan banter
- Social apps for the bigger game-day community

There is no single experience that connects all of these and makes the fan feel like part of the moment.

## The Solution
JETSYNC AI combines:
- Real-time game insights
- AI-generated commentary
- Fantasy intelligence
- AI watch party matchmaking
- AR touchdown experiences
- Personalized fan journey and predictions

## Key Features
1. Copilot Game Day Assistant
   - Fans can ask natural questions in real time
   - Answers explain drives, play outcomes, and strategic context

2. AI Watch Party Matchmaking
   - Find similar fans for shared game viewing
   - Match based on viewing style, fantasy interest, and location

3. Momentum Meter
   - Scores live momentum using possession, yardage, crowd energy, and game flow

4. AR Touchdown Celebration
   - Trigger fireworks, player highlights, and digital collectibles after a Jets score

5. Fantasy Command Center
   - Start/sit advice, injury insight, waiver suggestions, and matchups

6. AI Highlight Generator
   - Produce 60-second recaps for missed plays or moments

## Why It Matters
This product goes beyond score tracking. It creates a more emotional, social, and personalized fan experience that makes every Jets game feel more connected, smarter, and more exciting.

## Value Proposition
"Never watch a Jets game alone again."

JETSYNC AI turns each snap into a personalized fan moment—intelligent, social, and immersive.

## Business Model
- Free tier: live scores, AI commentary, basic insights
- Premium: $6.99/month for fantasy AI, watch-party matching, AR, advanced analytics
- Enterprise: sponsorship and stadium integration opportunities

## Target Audience
- New York Jets fans
- Fantasy football players
- Social sports fans
- Tailgaters and game-day community groups

## Team Pitch
JETSYNC AI is not just a sports app; it is an AI-driven fan companion that transforms game-day fandom into a connected, intelligent, and unforgettable experience.
